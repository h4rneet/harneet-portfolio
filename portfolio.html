Strawbeary Hollow

3D Modelling · Game Design · Immersive Environment Design

Strawbeary Hollow is a 3D immersive environment where the user plays as a bear named Berry who returns to her family's cottage to take a break from her stressful university life. The goal is to walk through the environment, interact with various objects that tell stories about Berry's life, and focus on the present.

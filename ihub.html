Innovation Hub

Graphic Design Visual Storytelling Communications Illustration

The Innovation Hub is a student-led design thinking collective at the University of Toronto that collaborates with campus partners to understand student stories through empathy and research, developing strategies that improve the student experience.

As a Graphic Designer and Communications Assistant, I contributed to an iterative and collaborative design process where each final visual reflected ideas and perspectives developed collectively by the team.

I designed graphics and icons following the Innovation Hub's design style and process, moving from moodboarding and sketching to layout exploration and digitization. Through visual storytelling, I helped translate information into clear, cohesive, and engaging graphics.
